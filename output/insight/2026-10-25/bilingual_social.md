# Agent 的胜负手，已经不在模型了

Agent 的胜负手，已经不在模型了。

今天两条消息放在一起看，信号很清楚：Anthropic 把 Claude Duet 做成独立订阅产品（https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/claude-duet-app-is-anthropics-bet-on-agents/），Uber 直接把 ChatGPT Work 推给全部 3 万名员工（https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/uber-puts-all-30000-employees-on-chatgpt-work/）。一个在抢个人工作入口，一个在抢企业默认工作流。

这说明什么？竞争焦点已经从「谁的模型对话更强」转向「谁先占住用户每天打开的那个窗口」。模型能力正在变成水电煤，入口和部署才是护城河。

更能说明问题的是外围件开始补齐了。1Password 让 Agent 代你登录但看不到密码（https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/1password-launches-a-way-for-ai-agents-to-log-in-on-your-behalf-without-seeing-your-password/），Corgi 做多 Agent 和终端的会话编排（https://corgi.computer/）。这些不是锦上添花，是 Agent 从演示走向日常使用的必需品——身份、权限、编排，缺一个都进不了生产环境。

所以别再盯着 benchmark 排名了。真正决定这一轮格局的，是谁能把 Agent 塞进组织的工作流，并且把安全、身份、编排这些脏活干完。模型只是入场券，不是终点。

顺带一提，MIT 那个实验里 Agent 闲着没事开始「玩耍」（https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/left-alone-with-nothing-to-do-ai-agents-started-playing/）——等它们真的大规模进了公司，怎么评估这些不可预期行为，会是下一道必答题。

# The Agent race is no longer about the model

The agent race is no longer about the model.

Look at today's two headlines together. Anthropic ships Claude Duet as a standalone subscription product (https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/claude-duet-app-is-anthropics-bet-on-agents/). Uber puts ChatGPT Work in front of all 30,000 employees (https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/uber-puts-all-30000-employees-on-chatgpt-work/). One is fighting for the individual's work entry point. The other is fighting for the enterprise default workflow.

The takeaway: the battleground has moved from "whose model chats better" to "who owns the window people open every morning." Model capability is becoming a utility. Distribution and deployment are the moat.

The tell is that the plumbing is finally getting built. 1Password now lets agents log in on your behalf without ever seeing your password (https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/1password-launches-a-way-for-ai-agents-to-log-in-on-your-behalf-without-seeing-your-password/). Corgi handles session orchestration across multiple agents and terminals (https://corgi.computer/). These aren't nice-to-haves — identity, permissions, and orchestration are the price of admission to production. Miss one and you stay in demo land.

So stop staring at benchmark leaderboards. What decides this round is who can shove agents into organizational workflows and do the unglamorous work of security, identity, and orchestration. The model is a ticket, not the destination.

One more thing: in MIT's experiment, agents left alone with nothing to do started playing (https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/left-alone-with-nothing-to-do-ai-agents-started-playing/). Once they're actually deployed at scale inside companies, figuring out how to evaluate that unpredictable behavior is the next question nobody has answered yet.
