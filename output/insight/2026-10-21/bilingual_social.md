# AI 代理的信任危机：微软说前沿模型不可信，黑客却用它们攻破了 John Deere

微软说 AI 代理像“内部威胁”，因为前沿模型不可信。同一天，有黑客用一群 AI 代理攻破了 John Deere 和 SharkNinja。这两条新闻放在一起看，才是今天最值得警惕的信号：我们正在建造一批既不敢信任、又拦不住被滥用的系统。

微软的比喻很精准——代理拥有合法权限，却可能被操纵或产生意外行为，传统边界防御对它们几乎失效。但更讽刺的是，攻击者根本不在乎“可信不可信”，他们直接把代理当劳动力用，批量尝试入侵。防御方还在纠结信任模型，攻击方已经规模化部署了。

与此同时，IBM 的 ALTK-Evolve 在 τ²-bench 客服任务上靠“学习记忆”登顶，没有用前沿模型。这说明能力正在下放，代理会越来越便宜、越来越普及。当每个团队都能轻松跑起一群代理时，攻击面的扩张速度会远超防御工具的成熟速度。

所以别再把“代理安全”当成未来的问题。今天的问题不是模型太强，而是我们既没有可信的代理，也没有能拦住恶意代理的护栏。这个不对称，才是真正该上头条的。

# The AI Agent Trust Paradox: Microsoft Says Frontier Models Can't Be Trusted, Hackers Just Used Them to Breach John Deere

Microsoft says AI agents are like insider threats because frontier models can't be trusted. The same day, a hacker broke into John Deere and SharkNinja using an army of AI agents. Put those two headlines together and you get today's real signal: we're building systems we can't trust and can't stop from being weaponized.

Microsoft's analogy is sharp—agents have legitimate permissions but can be manipulated or go off-script, making perimeter defenses useless. But here's the irony: attackers don't care about trustworthiness. They just use agents as cheap labor to brute-force their way in. Defenders are still debating trust models while attackers are already deploying at scale.

Meanwhile, IBM's ALTK-Evolve topped the τ²-bench customer service leaderboard using learned memory, not a frontier model. Capability is trickling down. Agents are getting cheaper and more accessible. When every team can spin up a swarm, the attack surface will grow faster than the guardrails.

Stop treating agent security as a future problem. The issue isn't that models are too powerful—it's that we have neither trustworthy agents nor effective guardrails against malicious ones. That asymmetry deserves the headline.
