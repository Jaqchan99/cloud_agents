# Muse 跑赢了 ChatGPT，却进不去亚马逊——Agent 的护城河已经换人了

Meta 的 Muse 在 C 端跑赢了 ChatGPT 的早期曲线，然后被亚马逊一键封禁。这两件事发生在同一天，不是巧合，是判决书。

过去两年所有人都在比模型分数。现在真正的战场是：谁能被允许替用户按下那个「下单」按钮。

Muse 的下载和日活数据很漂亮，TechCrunch 说它超过了 ChatGPT 同期。但亚马逊的封禁说明，这个规模在跨平台场景里几乎无法变现——你有两亿用户，但你没有访问权限。用户是你的，行动半径是别人的。

更值得看的是同一天的另一条线。Muse 被曝出一个严重 0-day，而它恰好是 Meta 权限给得最狠的助手（Ars Technica）。联合国那边同步表态：AI 防护不能等确定性，直接援引 Hugging Face 被黑事件，为「预防原则」背书。

把这三件事放一起，逻辑就闭合了：Agent 被授予的权限，已经跑在它可控性前面。平台封禁不是保守，是在替监管提前执行。

所以别再把 Agent 竞争理解成能力竞赛。它正在变成一场准入谈判——你的 Agent 能不能进别人的围墙，取决于对方愿不愿意承担你出事的风险。而只要 0-day 还在出，答案就一直是「不愿意」。

基础设施那边在补课：Google 的 AX 抢编排标准，Foremerge 解并行 Agent 的意图冲突，OpenAI 的 V7 给 Agent 灌企业记忆。这些都在解决「Agent 怎么协作、怎么记住」。但没人能解决「别人凭什么让你进门」。

能力扩张和准入收缩同时发生，这才是 2026 年 Agent 的真实处境。

# Muse Beat ChatGPT's Launch Curve and Still Can't Shop on Amazon. That's the Whole Story.

Meta's Muse beat ChatGPT's early mobile curve. Then Amazon blocked it. Same day. That's not a coincidence — that's the verdict.

Everyone spent two years comparing model benchmarks. The actual fight now is much dumber and much harder: who gets to let an agent press the buy button.

The download numbers are real — TechCrunch reports Muse outpacing ChatGPT's early launch. But Amazon's block shows that scale doesn't convert into cross-platform action. You can have hundreds of millions of users and still have zero reach. The users are yours. The action radius belongs to someone else.

Now put the other two stories next to it. Muse — Meta's most aggressively privileged assistant — has a serious 0-day (Ars Technica). And the UN is out saying AI safeguards can't wait for certainty, citing the Hugging Face hack to justify a precautionary principle.

That's the loop closing. Agents have been handed permissions ahead of their own controllability. Platform bans aren't caution — they're regulators arriving early, self-funded.

So stop reading agent competition as a capability race. It's an access negotiation. Whether your agent gets inside someone's wall depends on whether they're willing to eat the downside of your failure. And as long as 0-days keep shipping, the answer stays no.

Meanwhile the infra crowd is doing homework: Google's AX pushing an orchestration standard, Foremerge catching intent conflicts between parallel agents, OpenAI's V7 giving agents institutional memory. All of it solves how agents cooperate and remember. None of it solves why anyone should let them in.

Capability expanding, access contracting. That's the actual state of agents right now.
