# Agent 已经拿到身份证了，治理还停在会议室里

Agent 已经拿到身份证了，治理还停在会议室里。

这周最值得注意的不是某个模型又刷了什么榜，而是三件事同时发生：Meta 的 Muse 把个人 Agent 做成了消费级产品；Instinct 拿到了一个独立邮箱地址；Oto Dock 直接把 Claude Code 和 Codex 按部门塞进公司。三件事指向同一个事实——Agent 正在同时获得产品身份、操作身份和组织身份。

问题在于，身份一旦发出去，就收不回来了。

Instinct 有了邮箱，意味着它能替你注册账号、发申诉信、跟外部系统打交道。这是质变，不是量变：一个能被外部系统识别的 Agent，本质上就是一个新的法律和运营主体。可我们连「谁批准它代表我说话」这个问题都还没有答案。

更微妙的是 Meta 那出戏。Muse 乐队丢了社交账号，因为 Meta 的新 Agent 也叫 Muse。这不是巧合，这是产品发布作为基础设施级权力行使的现场演示——大厂连命名空间都不需要协商。一个连乐队名字都能顺手拿走的实体，你指望它在 Agent 身份治理上主动让权？

红杉这周加注 Cymphony 做非人类身份统一视图，Geiger 在本地扫描每个 Agent 能碰什么，这些方向都对。但它们是事后补救。真正的错配在于：Oto Dock 在鼓励你把更多 Agent 放进流程，Geiger 在限制它们能碰什么，而这两件事之间没有任何协调机制。扩张和收敛是同一个进程的两面，可行业现在把它们当成两个赛道。

我的判断很简单：2026 年下半年会有第一批因为 Agent 身份滥用而出的重大事故，而事故之后大家才会发现，缺的不是扫描工具，是「Agent 身份签发权归谁」这条最基本的规则。

工具在补，规则没写。这就是现在的全部真相。

# Agents Have ID Cards Now. Governance Is Still in the Conference Room.

Agents have ID cards now. Governance is still in the conference room.

The story this week isn't a benchmark. It's three things landing at once: Meta shipped Muse as a consumer personal agent, Instinct got its own email address, and Oto Dock started dropping Claude Code and Codex agents into company departments. Product identity, operational identity, organizational identity — all in the same news cycle.

Here's the part people are skipping: once you hand out an identity, you can't take it back.

Instinct having an email isn't a feature. It means the agent can register accounts, file appeals, negotiate with external systems on your behalf. That's a new operating entity, not a better chatbot. And we still don't have an answer to the most basic question: who authorized it to speak for me?

Then there's the Meta thing. Muse the band lost its handles to Muse the agent. That's not bad luck — that's a live demo of product launches functioning as infrastructure-level power. A company that can absorb a band's name without a conversation is not going to voluntarily cede authority over agent identity.

Sequoia doubling down on Cymphony for non-human identity visibility, Geiger scanning what every local agent can touch — right instincts, wrong timing. These are cleanups. The actual mismatch: Oto Dock is telling you to put more agents into workflows while Geiger is telling you to clamp down on what they reach. Two sides of one process, treated as two separate markets.

My call: the first serious agent-identity incident hits in the second half of 2026. And the postmortem won't say "we lacked scanning tools." It'll say we never decided who gets to issue an agent an identity.

The tools are catching up. The rules were never written. That's the whole story.
