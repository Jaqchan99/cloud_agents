# Agent 普及化是假象，垂直化才是真金

今天最值得注意的，不是 OpenAI 又要做“万能 Agent”，也不是微软开源了个框架。而是两条新闻放在一起，暴露了 Agent 赛道真正的分水岭：通用是故事，垂直是生意。

OpenAI 想把 Agent 铺给所有人（[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/24/openai-is-building-an-ai-agent-for-everything-will-everyone-use-them/)），微软用 Agent Lightning 降低门槛（[GitHub](https://github.com/microsoft/agent-lightning/releases/tag/v1.0.1)）——这俩是在建设基础设施，把 Agent 变成水电煤。但基础设施生意向来是巨头的，创业公司挤进去就是炮灰。

真正的信号在另一侧：DeepMind 校友创办的 Inherent，其 AI“同事”在复现科研任务上直接干翻了 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的通用模型（[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/22/inherent-founded-by-deepmind-alumni-says-its-ai-teammate-just-outperformed-anthropic-and-openai-at-replicating-research/)）。Valor 和 Point72 给具身智能公司 General Intuition 砸出 60 亿美元估值（[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/24/valor-point72-back-general-intuition-at-6b-valuation-as-ai-startup-pushes-into-robotics/)）。钱在往哪走？不是万能 Agent，是特定场景里能替代专家的 Agent。

通用 Agent 是所有人的梦想，但垂直 Agent 才是能签单、能收费、能建立壁垒的东西。OpenAI 和微软负责把路修好，而聪明的钱已经在垂直赛道上押注了。别被“全民化”的故事忽悠，问问自己：你的 Agent 在哪个领域能比 GPT 强 10 倍？如果没有，那它就没有存在的必要。

# The Agent Gold Rush Isn't About General-Purpose—It's About Vertical Dominance

The most telling news today isn't that OpenAI is building an agent for everything, or that Microsoft open-sourced another framework. It's what happens when you read them side by side: general-purpose agents are the narrative, vertical agents are the business.

OpenAI wants to put agents in everyone's hands ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/24/openai-is-building-an-ai-agent-for-everything-will-everyone-use-them/)), Microsoft's Agent Lightning lowers the barrier to entry ([GitHub](https://github.com/microsoft/agent-lightning/releases/tag/v1.0.1)). That's infrastructure play—turning agents into utilities. Infrastructure is a giant's game; startups trying to compete there are cannon fodder.

The real signal is on the other side: Inherent, founded by DeepMind alumni, claims its AI 'teammate' outperforms Anthropic and OpenAI at replicating research ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/22/inherent-founded-by-deepmind-alumni-says-its-ai-teammate-just-outperformed-anthropic-and-openai-at-replicating-research/)). Valor and Point72 just valued General Intuition at $6B for its robotics push ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/24/valor-point72-back-general-intuition-at-6b-valuation-as-ai-startup-pushes-into-robotics/)). Where's the money flowing? Not into universal agents—into agents that can replace experts in specific domains.

Universal agents are a dream; vertical agents are what gets you contracts, revenue, and a moat. OpenAI and Microsoft are building the highway, but the smart money is already betting on specialized vehicles. Don't get seduced by the 'democratization' story. Ask yourself: in what domain can your agent beat GPT by 10x? If the answer is nothing, it doesn't deserve to exist.
