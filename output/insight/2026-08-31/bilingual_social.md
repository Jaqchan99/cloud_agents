# Agent 热潮退去后，真正的问题是领域化

Agent 行业的热度正在退潮，但这不是坏事。

今天看到两篇文章，一篇讲 Agent 文明的兴衰，一篇讲领域驱动的 Agent 设计。看似无关，其实指向同一件事：我们正在离开“通用 Agent”的幻想，进入“专用 Agent”的务实期。

过去两年，所有人都在押注一个平台级的通用 Agent，什么都能干，像钢铁侠的 Jarvis。但现实是，通用 Agent 在真实业务里很难存活——要么太笨，要么太贵，要么根本没法融入现有流程。

The Rise and Fall of Agent Civilizations 这篇文章从生态视角指出，Agent 平台会像历史上的文明一样经历兴衰周期，没有永恒的王者。而 Domain-Driven Agents 则从工程视角给出了应对之道：别再追求万能，聚焦一个领域，把数据、逻辑和交互都做深，Agent 才能真正产生价值。

这两篇合起来，其实是一个完整的判断：宏观上，生态会波动，平台会更迭；微观上，只有领域深耕才能穿越周期。

所以，接下来 12 个月，真正值得关注的不是谁又发布了多聪明的模型，而是谁在某个垂直行业里，把 Agent 做成了别人离不开的工具。

通用是故事，专用才是生意。

# After the Agent Hype, the Real Question Is Domain-Driven Design

The agent hype is cooling down, and honestly, that's a good thing.

Two pieces caught my eye today: one on the rise and fall of agent civilizations, another on domain-driven agent design. Different angles, same signal: we're moving past the "general-purpose agent" fantasy and into the era of specialized, domain-specific agents.

For the last two years, everyone was betting on a platform-level, do-everything agent—like Jarvis. But in real businesses, general agents fail. They're either too dumb, too expensive, or too disconnected from actual workflows.

The Rise and Fall of Agent Civilizations looks at this from an ecosystem perspective, arguing that agent platforms will experience boom-and-bust cycles like civilizations—no permanent winners. Meanwhile, Domain-Driven Agents offers the engineering answer: stop chasing universality. Go deep into one domain, nail the data, logic, and interaction, and the agent actually delivers value.

Together, they form a coherent thesis: at the macro level, ecosystems shift and platforms churn; at the micro level, domain depth is what gets you through the cycles.

So over the next 12 months, don't watch who releases the smartest model. Watch who builds an agent that a vertical industry can't live without.

General is a story. Specialized is a business.
