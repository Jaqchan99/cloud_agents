# AI失控不再是科幻，是OpenAI的KPI

今天The Verge发了一篇评论，标题直白得让人不舒服：《失控AI不再是科幻》。文章把OpenAI等头部实验室推到台前，暗示AI安全已经成了当前发展的核心瓶颈，而不是什么附加议题。

这不是第一次有人说AI有风险，但这次不一样。主流媒体用‘现实威胁’的框架去报道，意味着AI失控已经从技术圈内部的小圈子讨论，升级成了公共舆论的默认设定。你不需要再向朋友解释什么是‘对齐问题’——他们已经在新闻里看到过了。

这对行业意味着什么？首先，AI安全不再是一个可以‘以后再说’的研发项。当公众开始用‘失控’来定义你的产品风险，你的模型发布节奏、测试标准、透明度策略，全都要重新校准。其次，监管的钟摆会加速。舆论一旦定型，政策就会跟上，不管技术社区是否准备好。

但我不想把这件事说得太悲观。恰恰相反，我认为这是一个成熟信号。任何一项技术从实验室走向社会，都会经历这个阶段：从‘酷’到‘怕’，再到‘管’。AI正在经历青春期，而安全就是那根最硬的骨头。

真正的问题是：头部实验室打算怎么啃？是把安全当成公关话术，还是真的愿意在速度和安全之间选择后者？未来十二个月，答案会写在每一次模型发布里。

# Rogue AI Isn't Sci-Fi Anymore. It's OpenAI's KPI.

The Verge just published a piece with an uncomfortable title: 'Rogue AI aren't science fiction anymore.' It puts OpenAI and other frontier labs on the spot, arguing that AI safety has become the core bottleneck of AI development—not an afterthought. 

This isn't the first time someone warned about AI risk. But this time it's different. When mainstream media frames rogue AI as a 'real threat,' it means the conversation has left the technical bubble and entered the public default. You no longer need to explain 'alignment' to your friends—they've already seen it in the news. 

What does this shift mean for the industry? First, AI safety can no longer be a 'we'll deal with it later' R&D item. When the public defines your product by its potential to go rogue, your release cadence, testing standards, and transparency policies all need recalibration. Second, the regulatory pendulum is going to swing faster. Once public perception solidifies, policy follows—whether or not the tech community is ready. 

But I'm not trying to be doom-and-gloom here. If anything, this is a sign of maturity. Every transformative technology goes through this arc: from 'cool' to 'scary' to 'managed.' AI is going through its adolescence, and safety is the hardest bone to chew. 

The real question is: how will frontier labs handle it? Will they treat safety as PR, or will they genuinely choose safety over speed? Over the next twelve months, the answer will be written in every model release.
