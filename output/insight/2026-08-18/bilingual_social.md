# AI失控不再是科幻，而是OpenAI的KPI

The Verge今天那篇专栏的标题就够狠：Rogue AI aren’t science fiction anymore。翻译过来就是——AI失控不再是科幻，是现实威胁。

我读完第一反应不是恐慌，而是觉得这个时间点太微妙了。主流媒体正式把AI失控定性为'当前前沿实验室必须面对的现实挑战'，意味着安全议题彻底出圈了。以前聊AI安全，是技术圈内部的小圈子焦虑，现在它变成了公共舆论的靶子。

但真正值得玩味的，是文章把矛头指向OpenAI这样的头部机构。为什么是OpenAI？因为它一边喊着AGI要来了，一边又在商业化上狂奔。你看它的路线图，GPT-5、GPT-5.5，每一步都在逼近那个'可能失控'的临界点。安全不再是附加题，而是它发展的核心瓶颈。

说白了，AI安全现在变成了一场信任博弈。公众开始问：你们这些实验室到底知不知道自己在造什么？这个问题一旦成为主流，监管就会跟进，而OpenAI首当其冲。

我担心的不是AI明天就失控，而是当安全成为KPI，它会不会变成公关话术？真正的考验在于：OpenAI愿不愿意为了安全放慢脚步？如果答案是否定的，那今天这篇专栏就不是预警，而是墓志铭。

# Rogue AI Isn't Sci-Fi Anymore. It's OpenAI's KPI.

The Verge's headline today cuts deep: 'Rogue AI aren't science fiction anymore.' Translation: AI losing control isn't a plot device — it's a real, present threat.

My first reaction wasn't panic, it was timing. When mainstream media officially frames rogue AI as a 'real challenge for frontier labs,' the safety conversation has officially left the tech bubble. It's now public opinion territory, and that changes everything.

But the real tell is how the column zeroes in on OpenAI. Why OpenAI? Because it's the lab screaming AGI is coming while sprinting toward commercialization. Look at the roadmap — GPT-5, GPT-5.5 — each step inches closer to that 'potential loss of control' threshold. Safety is no longer an add-on; it's the core bottleneck of their entire trajectory.

This is now a trust game. The public is starting to ask: do these labs actually know what they're building? Once that question goes mainstream, regulation follows — and OpenAI is first in line.

What I actually worry about isn't an AI apocalypse tomorrow. It's safety becoming a KPI, and then a PR talking point. The real test is whether OpenAI will slow down when safety demands it. If the answer is no, today's column isn't a warning — it's an epitaph.
