# AI 同事已上岗，但你的公司真的准备好了吗？

别再叫它们工具了，AI Agent 已经开始领工资了。

今天三条新闻拼在一起，信号不能再明显：General Catalyst 给成立仅两个月的 River AI 砸了 11 亿美元——一家连产品都没上线的公司，凭什么？凭的就是 Agentic AI 这个赛道已经火到资本闭眼投。

Grok 那边直接把自己重新定位成‘可分配工作的 AI 队友’，不是聊天机器人，是能接活、能交付的同事。而 OpenAI 的企业报告里，虽然讲的是从辅助到执行的转型故事，但关键数据其实在暗示：大多数企业还卡在‘辅助’阶段，真正敢把关键业务交给 Agent 的，寥寥无几。

这就形成了一个很有意思的错位：资本和产品在拼命往前冲，企业实际落地却还在爬坡。River AI 的估值、Grok 的定位，都是对未来的下注，而不是对现状的描述。

我的观点是：AI Agent 的范式转变是真的，但它不会像换软件一样平滑升级。它要求企业重新思考岗位职责、审批流程、责任归属——这些都不是技术问题，是组织问题。

所以别急着问‘该不该部署 Agent’，先问‘我的流程配得上 Agent 吗？’。

# AI Teammates Are Here, But Is Your Company Ready?

Stop calling them tools. AI agents are starting to earn a paycheck.

Today's news makes it unmistakable: General Catalyst just dropped $1.1B on River AI — a company that's two months old and hasn't even shipped a product. Why? Because the agentic AI space is so hot that VCs are betting blindfolded.

Meanwhile, Grok is repositioning itself as an "AI teammate you can assign work to" — not a chatbot, but a colleague that takes tasks and delivers. And OpenAI's enterprise report talks about the shift from assistance to execution, but the fine print suggests most companies are still stuck in the "assistance" phase. Very few have actually handed mission-critical work to agents.

Here's the disconnect: capital and products are sprinting ahead, while enterprise adoption is still crawling. River AI's valuation and Grok's positioning are bets on the future, not descriptions of the present.

My take: the paradigm shift is real, but it won't happen like a software upgrade. It demands rethinking job roles, approval chains, and accountability — those aren't tech problems, they're org problems.

So don't ask "should we deploy agents?" Ask "are our workflows even worthy of agents?"
